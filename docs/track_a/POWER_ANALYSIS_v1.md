# Power Analysis — LOEO CNP versus AdaCNP

> Generated by `scripts/power_analysis.py` from the executed **stage-5** sweep (408 runs, 17 folds).
> Noise is **measured**, not assumed. Regenerate with `python scripts/power_analysis.py`.

**Date:** 2026-07-30 — supersedes the stage-4 revision, which rested on three events

```
=== POWER ANALYSIS — leave-one-event-out CNP vs AdaCNP ===
  source                   stage 5 (full LOEO)
  runs                     408
  derived load-eligible events (= LOEO folds)   17
  seeds in the freeze      3
  alpha                    0.05    target power 0.8
  trials per estimate      6000

  Unit of analysis: per-event mean paired difference (seeds averaged).
  Per-fold normalization makes raw NLL incomparable across folds, so the
  paired within-event difference is the only admissible unit.

=== VARIANCE COMPONENTS, MEASURED FROM THE GRID ===
cell                      obs mean  sigma_event  sigma_seed  sigma_total
------------------------------------------------------------------------
base/nearest               +0.2309       0.5298      0.8640       1.0135
base/sampled               +0.0158       0.0000      1.1734       1.1734
temperature/nearest        -0.2403       0.6207      1.0058       1.1820
temperature/sampled        +0.4593       0.5703      0.6549       0.8684

  sigma_seed is initialisation noise and shrinks as 1/sqrt(seeds).
  sigma_event is between-event spread, reducible only by more events -- and
  the inventory is fixed by history.

  CAVEAT. sigma_event estimated as zero in 1 of 4 cells (base/sampled).
  The random-effects estimator subtracts the seed term from the observed
  between-event spread; where seed noise dominates, that difference goes
  negative and is clipped at zero. Read it as 'between-event spread is too
  small to resolve against the seed noise', NOT as 'events are
  interchangeable'. The MDE for those cells is correspondingly optimistic.

=== MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT at 17 events ===
cell                      MDE (1 seed)  MDE (3 seeds)  MDE (10 seeds)  as % of NLL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
base/nearest                    0.7285         0.5300          0.4311        16.3%
base/sampled                    0.8503         0.4901          0.2678        15.7%
temperature/nearest             0.8588         0.6152          0.5046        23.6%
temperature/sampled             0.6310         0.4947          0.4406        18.3%

  Seeds matter here. Because the measured noise is dominated by initialisation
  rather than by between-event spread, the detectable effect falls roughly as
  1/sqrt(events x seeds) -- so going from 3 to 10 seeds shrinks it by about a
  third, for a few minutes of extra CPU. The freeze fixes three seeds; on this
  evidence that is the cheapest available improvement to the design.
  (Subject to the sigma_event caveat above: if between-event spread is truly
  non-zero, extra seeds help less than these figures suggest.)

  'power now' is the three-event stage-4 design; 'power at 17' is the full
  LOEO sweep. Both assume the observed effect is the true one.

=== CONCLUSION ===
  1. At 17 events and 3 seeds the design detects a paired NLL
     difference of roughly 0.49 to 0.62 at 80% power.
  2. Hu et al.'s margin, converted to these units, is 0.02-0.12 -- an order of
     magnitude below that. Power to detect it is 0.05-0.15. A null stage-5
     result therefore says nothing about the paper's claim and must not be
     reported as failing to replicate it.
  3. But the effect actually observed in the temperature cells (~0.46) is
     large enough that the full sweep would detect it with 0.82-0.93 power. So
     stage 5 is worth running -- not to test the paper's effect size, but to
     test whether the larger effect seen here survives all the events.
  4. Raise the seed count. Initialisation noise dominates the measurement and
     is the one component still cheap to reduce.

  Whatever stage 5 reports, state the minimum detectable effect beside it.
  A difference smaller than the MDE is not evidence of absence.
```
